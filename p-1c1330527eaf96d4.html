Microfreak/UG/10 Keyboard/04 Octave Buttons

  • 10.4 Octave Buttons

    • The Octave buttons transpose the keyboard's output up or down by octaves.
    • The Octave Buttons
    • The buttons shift the pitch by up to three octaves in either direction. They blink faster as the shift moves farther from zero, reaching their fastest rate at -3 and +3 octaves. The two-octave keyboard can therefore cover an eight-octave pitch range.

    • Note/Info The octave shift is saved with the preset.

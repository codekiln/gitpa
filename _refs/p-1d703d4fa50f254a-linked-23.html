Logseq/Entity/Diataxis/Explanation

Page reference

Diataxis ExplanationWhat counts as an instance
  • Not an instance: a page of steps — Logseq/Entity/Diataxis/How To or Logseq/Entity/Diataxis/Tutorial. A systematic description of a thing — Logseq/Entity/Diataxis/Reference.
Diataxis Explanation

  • Relationship to Logseq/Entity/Concept

    • This quadrant overlaps the entity system almost entirely: an explanation-shaped page in this garden is normally a concept instance, and Logseq/Entity/Concept governs its frontmatter, its naming, and its body sections.
    • Read Logseq/Entity/Concept first, then this page for the Diataxis vocabulary and the tag. Where the two differ, the Concept page wins on shape and this page wins on which quadrant the writing belongs to.
    • Logseq/Entity/Term covers the narrower case of naming a thing rather than explaining it; a page can be both.
Diataxis ExplanationNaming and links
  • No namespace segment of its own — an explanation page is named for the idea it explains, wherever that idea naturally sits, per Logseq/Entity/Concept.
Diataxis ExplanationPage shape
  • Per Logseq/Entity/Concept. This type adds no shape of its own.
Diataxis ExplanationRelationship to other types
  • Logseq/Entity/Discussion — a Discussion page that weighs alternatives also carries this entity, after Discussion in its logseq-entity:: line. It takes its name, placement, shape and frontmatter from the Discussion definition, so an agent writing one leaves out the [[Diataxis/Concept]] tag that new Explanation pages otherwise carry.
Logseq/Entity/Discussion

Page reference

DiscussionWhat counts as an instance
  • The subject can be any page: an entity definition such as Logseq/Entity/Music/Scale, a principle such as My/Principle/Simplify/Prefer Standards and Defaults, or a preference such as My/Pref/Dev/mise/Tasks. The subject page may exist only as a logical page, as Logseq/Entity/Hierarchy does.
DiscussionWhat counts as an instance
  • Not an instance: a page about a conversation held somewhere else. A GitHub Discussions thread or a community forum thread is a Logseq/Entity/Forum/Post, even when its path contains Discussion, as mise/GitHub/Discussion/24/01/Vaults and Secrets Management does.
DiscussionNaming and placement
  • One Discussion page per subject, at <Subject>/Discussion: Logseq/Entity/Definition/Discussion discusses Logseq/Entity/Definition.
DiscussionFrontmatter
  • Logseq/Frontmatter/logseq-entity should include the Logseq/Entity/Discussion, as per Logseq/Entity standards.
DiscussionPage shape
  • H1 — the subject page's H1 followed by Discussion: - # Music Scale Discussion for Logseq/Entity/Music/Scale, whose H1 is Music Scale. When the subject has no page file, the H1 names the subject in Title Case without the Logseq/ prefix, as - # Entity Hierarchy Discussion does for Logseq/Entity/Hierarchy. Logseq/Entity/Definition/Discussion keeps the H1 codekiln wrote for it.
DiscussionPage shape
  • Years — every year, decade or century in the text links to its Logseq/Entity/Time/Year page, named by digit groups: the year 1547 is 15/4/7 and the twentieth century is 19, written in a sentence as [twentieth century]([[19]]).
DiscussionPage shape
  • A Discussion page may hold only its first line and its ## Entries section, as Logseq/Entity/Hierarchy/Discussion does.
DiscussionDated analyses and entriesAn analysis is one author's written reasoning about the subject at one time, usually an AI model's. Each analysis has its own page, nested under the Discussion it belongs to:
  • <Model> is the last segment of the author's Logseq/Entity/AI/Model page: Opus for Anthropic/Model/Claude/5/5/Opus, Fable for Anthropic/Model/Claude/5/1/Fable.
DiscussionDated analyses and entries
  • Logseq/Entity/Hierarchy/Discussion is the worked example: its entry for 2026-10-01 Thu links Logseq/Entity/Hierarchy/Discussion/Analysis/Opus/26/10/01/0611 ET Mark each page with the most specific entity in each lineage, and a nested bullet records the property codekiln proposed while reviewing it.
Discussion

  • Combined with Logseq/Entity/Diataxis/Explanation

    • A Discussion page whose sections give reasons, history and the alternatives weighed is also a Diataxis Explanation, and lists Discussion first: logseq-entity:: [[Logseq/Entity/Discussion]], [[Logseq/Entity/Diataxis/Explanation]].
    • The Discussion definition sets the page's name, placement, shape and frontmatter. An agent writing a Discussion page leaves out tags::, including the [[Diataxis/Concept]] tag the Explanation definition asks for, because the always-on logseq-core rule bars agents from adding, changing or removing tags:: on any page.
    • A Discussion page made only of entries carries Discussion alone.
DiscussionExamples in this garden
  • Logseq/Entity/Definition/Discussion, Logseq/Entity/Music/Scale/Discussion — combined with Explanation.
DiscussionExamples in this garden
  • Logseq/Entity/Hierarchy/Discussion — entries only.

Frontmatter DefinitionWhat counts as an instance

Frontmatter DefinitionFrontmatter
  • On instances, set alias:: [[<key>]] to the literal key the page defines, as alias:: [[logseq-entity-hierarchy-type]] on Logseq/Entity/Hierarchy/Frontmatter/Type. Clicking a key in <key>:: <value> opens the page [[<key>]], and the alias makes that page the definition.
Frontmatter DefinitionRelationships to other types

Record LabelWhat counts

Record LabelRelationships

Page reference

Music RecordingNaming and placement
  • Place a recording under its first credited Logseq/Entity/Identity/Music as <Identity>/Music/Recording/<Title> when Logseq/Frontmatter/created-by-identities is present. For a joint release, keep one page under the first credited identity and list all credited identities. Without a separate identity, use Person/<Artist>/Music/Recording/<Title>. Existing group namespaces remain valid until deliberately renamed.
Music RecordingRelationships

Music RecordingInstance shape

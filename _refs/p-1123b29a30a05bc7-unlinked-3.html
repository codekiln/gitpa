Launchpad/UG/10 Setup/08 Bootloader Menu
  • Hold Setup while plugging in Launchpad Pro to open the bootloader menu.
  • Set a different Device ID on each Launchpad Pro when using several with Live. Each unit then has an independent Session View outline.
  • MSD Mode controls whether the device appears as a mass storage drive. It is on by default; the bright pad means enabled and the dim pad means disabled. The LAUNCHPAD drive contains a link to the Easy Start Tool.

Music/Scale/Marwa
  • Spelled Marva on the Launchpad. In sargam: S r G M' P D N. On C: C D♭ E F♯ G A B. Steps in semitones: 1 3 2 1 2 2 1.
  • Hindustani pitches are tuned by ear against the tanpura drone and do not match equal temperament. The equal-tempered spelling on a Launchpad approximates the collection without its intonation or melodic rules.
Music/Scale/Minor/Natural
  • The MicroFreak's "Minor" setting is listed in its manual as C D E♭ F G A B♭ B, which lacks A♭, the note that separates natural minor from Dorian, and adds B natural. See Microfreak/UG/15 Using Scales/01 Scale Settings and Music/Scale/Dorian/Bebop. The Launchpad's "Natural Minor" setting carries this scale's name; its manual lists no pitches.

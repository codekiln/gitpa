Music/Scale/Blues
Blues Scale
  • Also called the minor blues or hexatonic blues scale. On C, in its standard textbook form: C E♭ F G♭ G B♭. Steps in semitones: 3 2 1 1 3 2. Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Minor with an added ♭5.
Music/Scale/Minor/Hungarian
Hungarian Minor ScaleHistory
  • Person/Béla Bartók and Person/Zoltán Kodály's fieldwork after 1905 found that older Hungarian peasant song was largely pentatonic. The "Hungarian" in the scale's name therefore names an urban, Romani-performed popular style rather than Hungarian village song. See Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Minor.
Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Major
Major Pentatonic ScaleUse
  • Country, gospel and rock guitar use the major pentatonic for major-key soloing. Its five modes include Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Minor.

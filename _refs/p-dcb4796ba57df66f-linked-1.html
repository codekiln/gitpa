Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/03 Transport and Playback
6.3 Transport and Playback
  • Playback starts from the flashing blue Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Marker/Insert Marker, at the Arrangement start by default. Click in a track to move it; double-click Stop, or press Home (Win) / Fn+Left (Mac), to send it back to the start.
Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/05 Time Signature Changes
6.5 Time Signature Changes
  • Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Marker/Time Signature Markers change the meter anywhere. Insert one at the insert marker with Create → Insert Time Signature Change or the scrub area's context menu.
Ableton/Live/UG/06 Arrangement View/06 The Arrangement Loop
6.6 The Arrangement Loop
  • Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Toggle/Arrangement Loop repeats part of the Arrangement. With nothing selected, the Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Marker/Loop Brace spans the whole Arrangement.

Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Button/Play
Play button
  • Starts Arrangement playback from the Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Marker/Insert Marker. The space bar toggles playback; Shift+space continues from where playback last stopped.
Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Field/Arrangement Position
Arrangement Position field
  • Shows and sets the play position in bars-beats-sixteenths; changing it moves the Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Marker/Insert Marker. Drag up or down, type a number and press Enter, or use the up and down arrow keys.
Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Field/Loop Length
Loop Length field
  • Sets the length of the Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Marker/Loop Brace, in bars-beats-sixteenths.
Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Field/Loop Start
Loop Start field
  • Sets where the Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Marker/Loop Brace starts, in bars-beats-sixteenths. Also the punch-in point.
Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Switch/Punch-In
Punch-In switch
  • Behavior — toggle. The points follow the Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Marker/Loop Brace.
Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Switch/Punch-Out
Punch-Out switch
  • Behavior — toggle. The points follow the Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Marker/Loop Brace.
Ableton/Live/UI/Control Bar/Toggle/Arrangement Loop
Arrangement Loop toggle
  • Repeats the section under the Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Marker/Loop Brace. With nothing selected, the brace covers the whole Arrangement. Cmd+L (Mac) / Ctrl+L (Win) loops the current selection.

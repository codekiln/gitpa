Music/Scale/Hirajoshi
Hirajoshi ScaleQuirks
  • It is a hemitonic pentatonic, the opposite of Music/Anhemitonic: two of its five steps are semitones, each followed by a major third. That pairing gives it a sound unlike the semitone-free Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Major.
Music/Scale/Major
Major ScaleQuirks
  • The pitch-class complement of the major scale is Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Major: on a piano, white keys against black keys.
Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Minor
Minor Pentatonic Scale
  • Also called la-pentatonic in Kodály solmization and yu mode in Chinese theory. On C: C E♭ F G B♭. Steps in semitones: 3 2 2 3 2. The fifth mode of Music/Scale/Pentatonic/Major.

Logseq/Entity/Music/Performance
  • Place a performance under its principal performer as Person/<Performer>/Music/Performance/<Date or Event>/<Work>.
  • Use work:: for the performed work, performer:: for performers as defined at Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording/Frontmatter/performer, and recording:: for a recording page that documents the event.
Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording
  • Use Logseq/Frontmatter/created-by-identities for published artist credits; created-by:: and performer:: retain verified personal credits rather than being inferred from identity membership.
  • Add performer::, date-created::, and links to the represented work, performance, or release when those details are known.

Logseq/Entity/Music/Work
  • Person/Stevie Nicks/Music/Work/Landslide is the song. A Smashing Pumpkins recording of it is filed under that performer and links to the Stevie Nicks work with cover-of::.
Logseq/Frontmatter/created-by-identities
  • A name that is only a person's ordinary biographical name can use created-by:: or performer:: directly without a separate identity page.
  • created-by:: records known people who made the particular work or recording; performer:: records known performers. Neither property is inferred from identity membership alone.

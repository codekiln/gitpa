Microfreak/Preset/369 TMF.24.11.23.C

Page reference

Notes
  • An archived GitP.24.11.23.C.mfpz patch contains this preset name. It shares its stored digital oscillator Type and two oscillator parameter fields with the archived Microfreak/Preset/368 TMF.24.11.23.B and Microfreak/Preset/370 TMF.24.11.23.D patches. Compared with B, the remaining oscillator field, filter cutoff, and resonance differ. Compared with D, a different oscillator field and the filter cutoff differ, while resonance matches. Microfreak/UG/06 Dig Osc/02 Param Controls describes Type and its three shaping controls.

Notes

Microfreak/UG/01 Welcome and Introduction

Page reference

01 Welcome and Introduction
  • The standard MicroFreak and MicroFreak Vocoder Edition share the instrument's core features. The Vocoder Edition has a white casing and a gooseneck microphone; see Microfreak/UG/19 Appendix B Vocoder for its specific features.
01 Welcome and Introduction
  • Microfreak/UG/01 Welcome and Introduction/01 A Fascinating Adventure

    • 1.1 A fascinating adventure

      • Learning the MicroFreak comes through experimenting, asking questions, and trying ideas. Take time to understand its functions one by one; random tweaking without knowing what is changing can make it harder to stay interested.
      • Test what you learn as you go. Understanding the instrument helps you create the sounds you imagine.
01 Welcome and Introduction
  • Microfreak/UG/01 Welcome and Introduction/02 New in FW 5.0.0

    • 1.2 New in firmware (FW) 5.0.0

      • Firmware 5.0.0 adds:
        • Four sample-based oscillators: Sample, Scan Grains, Cloud Grains, and Hit Grains.
        • A Samples tab in MIDI Control Center.
        • A preset bank with 52 factory samples. The new presets and samples are available in the latest MIDI Control Center.
        • Expanded preset storage for up to 512 presets.
        • An optimized Utility menu.
01 Welcome and Introduction
  • Microfreak/UG/01 Welcome and Introduction/03 About Reading Manuals

    • 1.3 About reading manuals

      • Reading a manual can build more than familiarity with an instrument. Each pass adds knowledge that can connect with what you already know and prompt new ideas.
      • A first reading introduces the parameters and their effects. Later readings clarify the instrument's structure; returning to the material can offer fresh creative starting points.

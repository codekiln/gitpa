Music Scale DiscussionA raga has a page of its own
  • V. N. Bhatkhande grouped Hindustani ragas under ten parent scales, or thaats, in the early twentieth century. A thaat is a pitch collection, so each thaat gets a scale page, as Music/Scale/Marwa and Music/Scale/Todi do, and a raga page links to the scale page of its parent thaat.

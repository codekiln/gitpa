Other settings
  • Zoom H5/UG/13 Settings/12 Format SD

    • Formatting SD cards

      • Format an SD card in the H5 to prepare it for use.
      • 1. Press MENU, then use the scroll button to select SD CARD > FORMAT.
      • 2. At Are you sure?, select YES and press the scroll button.
        • SD card format screen

      • Note/Info Format a newly bought SD card, or one formatted on a computer, in the H5 before using it.

      • Note/Warning Formatting deletes all data on the SD card.

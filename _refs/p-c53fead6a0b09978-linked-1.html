Major ScaleQuirks
  • Inverting the step pattern about the tonic produces Music/Scale/Phrygian. Inverting Mixolydian produces Music/Scale/Minor/Natural, and Music/Scale/Dorian inverts to itself.
Music/Scale/Todi
Todi ThaatQuirks
  • The name points to two different scales. Carnatic Todi, Hanumatodi or melakarta 8, has the notes of Music/Scale/Phrygian, while Hindustani Todi has a raised fourth and a raised seventh.

Logseq/Entity/UI/Area

Page reference

UI AreaWhat counts
  • <Item>/UI is the root area for an item and covers its whole interface, the way <Item>/UG holds its Logseq/Entity/Book/User Guide.
UI AreaWhat counts
  • A single knob, button, pad or field is a Logseq/Entity/UI/Control, not an area.
UI AreaAtomic Design stages
  • A Logseq/Entity/UI/Control corresponds to an UX/Design/Atomic/L1 - Atom. A preset or a saved Live Set, which fills the interface with real values, corresponds to an UX/Design/Atomic/L5 - Page and is modeled by its own entity.
UI AreaIdentity and names
  • Inside a row, or on a side without rows, each item takes a two-digit number counting left to right: 01 Matrix, 02 Paraphonic Button. A numbered group of controls is an area, such as Microfreak/UI/Front/Row/01 Top/01 Matrix; a numbered single control is a Logseq/Entity/UI/Control.
UI AreaPage shape
  • A ## Controls section links each Logseq/Entity/UI/Control in the area, in panel or screen order. A ## Areas section links smaller areas when there are any. Link rather than embed; control pages are short and many.

Logseq/Frontmatter/created-by-identities
created-by-identities:: — credited public identities
  • On a work, recording or other creation, lists the Logseq/Entity/Identity pages credited as its creators or artists, in published order.
created-by-identities:: — credited public identitiesPlacement
  • The first credited identity supplies the namespace when the creation's entity definition uses identity-based placement. Logseq/Entity/Music/Recording and Logseq/Entity/Music/Work define musical placement.
Logseq/Frontmatter/logseq-created-time-year
  • The value is a link to the matching Logseq/Entity/Time/Year instance, at whatever precision is actually known — a specific year, or its decade or century when only that much is known.
  • Distinct from date-created::: that property holds the display-quality date value (day, month, or year, per Logseq/Date) and does not always resolve to a Logseq/Entity/Time/Year page. logseq-created-time-year:: always does, so every entity created in a given year, decade, or century collects there as a backlink regardless of the entity's own type.
Logseq/Frontmatter/logseq-entity
logseq-entity:: marks a page as an instance of a Logseq entity
  • The value should be the relevant entity definition page, such as Logseq/Entity/Game, Logseq/Entity/Software/Project, or Logseq/Entity/Question.
logseq-entity:: marks a page as an instance of a Logseq entity
  • A page that models more than one entity lists each marker, comma-separated, primary entity first. Logseq/Entity describes multiple entity membership.
Logseq/Frontmatter/tags
tags::- treat Logseq/Tags with care
  • Logseq tags have a special impact on the Logseq UI, but their implementation is buggy and inconsistent. In addition, the human gardener's use of tags:: pre-dated the more mature Logseq/Entity entity system, and the human's rules for applying them are fuzzy and ill-defined. It's easier to use them incorrectly than to use them correctly.

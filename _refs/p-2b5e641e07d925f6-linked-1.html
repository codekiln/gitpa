Logseq/Entity/UI/Area
UI AreaIdentity and names
  • A hardware panel can also be mapped by position. Each side of the device is an area, <Item>/UI/Front or <Item>/UI/Rear; a side laid out in rows has one area per row at <Item>/UI/Front/Row/<NN Row>, numbered top to bottom, such as Microfreak/UI/Front/Row/02 Main.
UI AreaIdentity and names
  • Inside a row, or on a side without rows, each item takes a two-digit number counting left to right: 01 Matrix, 02 Paraphonic Button. A numbered group of controls is an area, such as Microfreak/UI/Front/Row/01 Top/01 Matrix; a numbered single control is a Logseq/Entity/UI/Control.
UI AreaExamples
  • Microfreak/UI/Front → Microfreak/UI/Front/Row/01 Top → Microfreak/UI/Front/Row/01 Top/01 Matrix
Logseq/Entity/UI/Control
UI ControlIdentity and names
  • On a panel mapped by position, a numbered single control carries its kind in its own name, <Row>/<NN> <Name> <Kind>, as in Microfreak/UI/Front/Row/01 Top/02 Paraphonic Button. A control in a numbered group keeps <Group>/<Kind>/<Name>, and when the group has only one control of that kind the name is left off: Microfreak/UI/Front/Row/01 Top/01 Matrix/Knob.
Logseq/Entity/UI/Control/Setting
UI Control SettingWhat counts
  • A gesture: a way of operating the control other than its plain use, such as pressing a knob, holding a button, or using the control while Shift is held: Microfreak/UI/Front/Row/01 Top/06 Save Button/Hold.
UI Control SettingIdentity and names
  • A gesture sits at <Control>/<Gesture>, named Press, Hold or Shift. A gesture that combines two nests the second beneath the first: Microfreak/UI/Front/Row/01 Top/02 Paraphonic Button/Hold/Shift.

- # [35.6.1 Adjusting the Loop Length](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-1/#adjusting-the-loop-length)
	- Each loop length pad is a page of steps; a page's length depends on the step size. Drums at 16ths show two pages, or two bars; the Melodic Sequencer shows one page of eight steps, or two beats.
	- Hold one pad and tap another to set the loop; double-tap a pad for a one-page loop.
	- Pages follow the play position by default. Single-tap a page to lock the view to it while the loop keeps its length; reselect the loop to follow again. Tapping a page outside the loop sets the loop to it.
	- Colors: unlit is outside the loop, white is in the loop but not shown, dull yellow is shown but not playing, green is playing.
	- `Shift` + Note locks the pads per track. Hold Duplicate and press two page pads to copy a page on top of the destination; hold Delete and tap a page pad to clear it first.

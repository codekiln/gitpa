- # [26.9.2 Locking Envelopes in Automation Lanes](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/automation/#locking-envelopes-in-automation-lanes)
	- Moving an Arrangement clip normally moves its automation. [[Ableton/Live/UI/Arrangement View/Toggle/Lock Envelopes]], or Options → Lock Envelopes, keeps automation at its timeline position instead.
		- ![The Lock Envelopes toggle](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/LockEnvelopesToggleL12.png?auto=&w=320)

- # [42.16 Arrangement View](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-keyboard-shortcuts/#arrangement-view)
	- Split Clip at Selection — `Cmd+E` (Win `Ctrl+E`)
	- Consolidate Selection into Clip — `Cmd+J` (Win `Ctrl+J`)
	- Crop Selected Clips — `Cmd+Shift+J` (Win `Ctrl+Shift+J`)
	- Resize Clip When Insert Marker is at Clip Edge — `Enter` left and right arrow keys
	- Slide Waveform — `Shift+Option` drag (Win `Shift+Alt` drag)
	- Stretch Warped Clip — `Shift` drag in clip title bar
	- Select Time Within Clip — `Shift+Option` drag in clip title bar (Win `Shift+Alt` drag in clip title bar)
	- Create Fade/Crossfade — `Cmd+Option+F` (Win `Ctrl+Alt+F`)
	- Delete Fades/Crossfades in Selected Clip(s) — `Cmd+Option+Delete` (Win `Ctrl+Alt+Backspace`)
	- Momentarily Toggle Fade Handles — `F`
	- Toggle Loop Brace — `Cmd+L` (Win `Ctrl+L`)
	- Adjust Loop Brace Length — `Cmd` left and right arrow keys (Win `Ctrl` left and right arrow keys)
	- Select Loop Brace Contents — `Cmd+Shift+L` (Win `Ctrl+Shift+L`)
	- Insert Silence — `Cmd+I` (Win `Ctrl+I`)
	- Cut Time — `Cmd+Shift+X` (Win `Ctrl+Shift+X`)
	- Copy Time — `Cmd+Shift+C` (Win `Ctrl+Shift+C`)
	- Paste Time — `Cmd+Shift+V` (Win `Ctrl+Shift+V`)
	- Duplicate Time — `Cmd+Shift+D` (Win `Ctrl+Shift+D`)
	- Delete Time — `Cmd+Shift+Delete` (Win `Ctrl+Shift+Delete`)
	- Fold/Unfold Selected Tracks — `U` or left and right arrow keys
	- Unfold All Tracks — `Option+U` (Win `Alt+U`)
	- Adjust Height of Selected Tracks/Clips — `Option` `+` and `Option` `-` (Win `Alt` `+` and `Alt` `-`)
	- Scroll Display to Follow Playback — `Option+Shift+F` (Win `Ctrl+Shift+F`)
	- Scroll Display Left/Right of Selection — `Cmd+Option` drag (Win `Ctrl+Alt` drag)
	- Optimize Arrangement Height — `H`
	- Optimize Arrangement Width — `W`
	- Deactivate Selection — `0`
	- Nudge Selection Left/Right — right and left arrow keys
	- Reverse Audio Clip Selection — `R`
	- Zoom to Arrangement Time Selection — `Z`
	- Zoom Back from Arrangement Time Selection — `X`
	- Play from Insert Marker in Selected Clip — `Option+Space` (Win `Ctrl+Space`)
	- Move Insert Marker to Playhead Position — `Cmd+Shift+Space` (Win `Ctrl+Shift+Space`)
	- Jump to Highlighted Track Title Bar — `Esc`
	- Move Focus to Mixer — `Option+Shift+M` (Win `Alt+Shift+M`)

- # [36.9 Navigating in Note Mode](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-2/#navigating-in-note-mode)
	- Jump to a track with its lower display button, or step with the left and right arrows.
		- ![Arrow buttons](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2Arrows.png?auto=&w=281)
	- Selecting a MIDI track on Push 2 arms it so you can play right away; its Arm button turns pink in Live.
	- Hold a track's lower display button, or Record plus that button, to arm it manually (red in Live, with an icon on Push 2's display) — for recording audio, for instance.
	- Up/Down move one scene. In Scene Workflow (Setup menu) they launch the scene; in Clip Workflow only the selected track's clip. Playback starts immediately and takes over the play position, as with Legato Mode in Live.

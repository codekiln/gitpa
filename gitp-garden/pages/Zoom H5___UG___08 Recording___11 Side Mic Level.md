- # Adjusting the side mic level
	- This works only with an input capsule whose side mic level (stereo width) can be adjusted, such as an MS capsule. Set the level before recording.
	- 1. Move the scroll button up or down to adjust the side mic level.
		- The settings are `OFF`, −24 to +6 dB, and `RAW` (MS-RAW mode). Higher values give a wider stereo image, and the icon changes with the value.
		- Side mic level display, from `RAW` and the widest setting down to `OFF`
			- ![01 Side mic level display](https://s3.us-east-005.backblazeb2.com/logseq-encode-garden/Zoom%20H5___UG___08%20Recording___11%20Side%20Mic%20Level___Asset___01-Side-mic-level-display.png)
	- > [[Note/Info]] For a recording made in MS-RAW mode, move the scroll button up or down during playback to adjust the side mic level.
	- > [[Note/Info]] An MS-RAW file is not an ordinary stereo file. To use it with other equipment, convert it to normal stereo with ZOOM MS Decoder or similar software.
	- > [[Note/Info]] MS-RAW mode is available only when recording in WAV format.

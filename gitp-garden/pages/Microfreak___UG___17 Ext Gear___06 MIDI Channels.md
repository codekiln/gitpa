- # 17.5 About MIDI channels
	- A central synthesizer or MIDI controller can control several instruments. MIDI has 16 channels so one device can send separate parts to other instruments on different channels.
	- The MicroFreak can transmit on all 16 channels at once or on a selected channel. By default, it transmits on all channels. To send a performance or sequence to a particular synthesizer or drum machine, set the MicroFreak's output channel to match; drum machines commonly use channel 10. Change the channel in Utility or MIDI Control Center.
	- MIDI input works the same way. To have a controller such as an Arturia BeatStep Pro control the MicroFreak on channel 2, set its input channel to 2 in Utility.

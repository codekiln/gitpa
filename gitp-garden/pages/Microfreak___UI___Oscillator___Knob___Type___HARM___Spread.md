- # Spread (Harm)
	- Set by the [[Microfreak/UI/Oscillator/Knob/Wave]] while the oscillator model is [[Microfreak/UI/Oscillator/Knob/Type/HARM]].
	- Sets the relation between partials. At zero they are in unison; at maximum they are an octave apart. Intermediate positions interpolate linearly in frequency.

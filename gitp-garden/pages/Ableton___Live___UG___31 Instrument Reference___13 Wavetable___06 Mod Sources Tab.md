- # [31.13.6 Mod Sources Tab](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#mod-sources-tab)
	- Settings for the envelopes and LFOs.
		- ![Wavetable's Mod Sources Tab](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/WavetableModSourcesTabL12.png)
	- ## Envelopes
		- **Amp**, **Env 2** and **Env 3**, edited by value or by dragging the display.
		- **Attack**, **Decay**, **Sustain** (multiplicative) and **Release**, each with a slope: positive moves fast then slow, negative slow then fast, zero linear.
		- Env 2 and Env 3 add **Initial** (multiplicative), **Peak** and **Final**.
		- **Loop Modes** — None holds sustain; Trigger plays all segments once; Loop repeats the whole envelope until the voice ends.
	- ## 31.13.6.1 LFOs
		- ![Wavetable's LFOs](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/WavetableLFOsL12.png)
		- Five waveforms, each reshaped by **Shape**: Sine and Saw tilt the slope; Triangle morphs ramp to saw; Square sets pulse width; Random changes how often extreme values occur.
		- **Sync** and **Rate**, or drag the display. **Amount** (multiplicative), **Offset** (phase, not modulatable), **Attack** (fade-in) and **Retrigger**.

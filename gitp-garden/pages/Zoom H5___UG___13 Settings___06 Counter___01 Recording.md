- # Set how time is shown during recording
	- While recording, the counter can show the elapsed recording time (count up) or the recording time still available (count down).
	- 1. Press **MENU**, then use the scroll button to select `REC > COUNTER SETUP`.
	- 2. Select `COUNT UP` or `COUNT DOWN` and press the scroll button.

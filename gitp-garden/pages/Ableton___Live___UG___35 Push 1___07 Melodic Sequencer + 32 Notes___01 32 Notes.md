- # [35.7.1 32 Notes](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-1/#32-notes)
	- The bottom half plays scale notes and selects them for sequencing; selected notes show in a lighter track color. Hold Select to select without playing.
	- Colors: blue is the root, blue-green is selected, green is playing, white is in scale.
	- Octave Up and Down shift the range; `Shift` with the touch strip moves by octaves and `Shift` with Octave moves by one scale note. The Scale menu applies here too.

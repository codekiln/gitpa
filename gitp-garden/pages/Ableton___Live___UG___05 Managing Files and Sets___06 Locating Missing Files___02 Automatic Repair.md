- # [5.6.2 Automatic Repair](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/managing-files-and-sets/#automatic-repair)
	- The Automatic Search Go button searches for missing files; its triangle shows options to search a chosen folder, the Project folder, and the Library.
	- No candidate: try another folder or repair by hand. One: accepted. Several: press the line's Hot-Swap button and double-click candidates in the browser to choose, even while the music plays.

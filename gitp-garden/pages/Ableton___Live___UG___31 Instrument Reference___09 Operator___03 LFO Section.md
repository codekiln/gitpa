- # [31.9.3 LFO Section](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#lfo-section-1)
	- The LFO runs up to audio rate, so it works almost like a fifth oscillator modulating the others' frequency.
		- ![Operator's LFO Display and Shell Parameters](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/OperatorLfoL12.png)
	- **Dest. A** buttons send it to each oscillator and the filter, with one amount; **Dest. B** adds another target with its own amount.
	- Waveforms: classic shapes, **S&H** for random steps, and band-pass filtered **Noise**. The manual's tip: LFO noise is the key to FM hi-hats and snares.
	- **Rate** with a Low, High or Sync range; **Rate < Key** makes it follow pitch; **R** retriggers.
	- **LFO Amount** scales both destinations, modulated by **Amt < Vel** and the LFO's envelope.

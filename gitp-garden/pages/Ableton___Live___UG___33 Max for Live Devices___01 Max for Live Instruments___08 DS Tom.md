- # [33.1.8 DS Tom](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/max-for-live-devices/#ds-tom)
	- ![The DS Tom Instrument](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/MaxDSTomL12.png?auto=&w=314)
	- Toms from an impulse and pitched oscillator waves, from deep to sharp.
	- **Pitch** — tuning in Hz
	- **Color** — filter gain and cutoff
	- **Tone** — level of resonant band-pass filters that imitate a drum head's tuning
	- **Bend** — pitch envelope
	- **Decay** (length) and **Volume**

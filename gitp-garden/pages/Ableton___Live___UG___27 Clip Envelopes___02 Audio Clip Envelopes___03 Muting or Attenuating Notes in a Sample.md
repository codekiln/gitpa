- # [27.2.3 Muting or Attenuating Notes in a Sample](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/clip-envelopes/#muting-or-attenuating-notes-in-a-sample)
	- Choose Clip → Gain and draw any volume shape onto the sample.
		- ![Imposing a volume envelope on a sample](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/VolumeEnvAndResult.png?auto=&w=320)
	- The envelope is a percentage of the Clip Gain setting, so it can turn notes down to silence but never above the gain slider.

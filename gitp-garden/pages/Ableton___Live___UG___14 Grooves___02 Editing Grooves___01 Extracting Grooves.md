- # [14.2.1 Extracting Grooves](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-grooves/#extracting-grooves)
	- Drag any audio or MIDI clip into the Groove Pool, or choose Extract Groove from its context menu, to make a groove from its timing and volume.
		- ![Extracting grooves from audio or MIDI clips](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/ExtractGroove.png?auto=&w=320)
	- Only the playing part of the clip counts.

- # [31.9.8 Strategies for Saving CPU Power](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#strategies-for-saving-cpu-power)
	- Turn off unused features, especially the filter and LFO, and keep voices at 6 to 12. Use Spread sparingly, and switch off Interpolation and Antialias if needed.
	- Turning off oscillators doesn't save CPU.

- # 16. Paraphonic Chord Mode
	- Paraphonic Chord Mode lets you transpose a chord through the current scale.
	- ## Initiate Paraphonic Chord Mode
		- Press the Paraphonic button. It blinks while the mode is active.
		- Hold the Paraphonic button and play a chord.
		- Lift your fingers from the keyboard, then release the Paraphonic button. The button stays on and blinks slowly.
		- Play a key to hear the chord with the same interval structure, transposed to that key and fitted to the current scale.
	- A chord's intervals come from scale steps. The root is its first note. A third three semitones above the root makes a minor chord; a third four semitones above makes a major chord. Adding notes shapes the chord further. Paraphonic Chord Mode analyzes the intervals in the chord you play, then rebuilds those intervals from each key you play. For example, a minor seventh chord becomes a minor seventh chord rooted at the played key.
	- Paraphonic Chord Mode can turn scale-quantized chords into fast, intricate arpeggios.
	- Press the Paraphonic button again to end Paraphonic Chord Mode.

## `logseq-entity::` marks a page as an instance of a Logseq entity
	- The value should be the relevant entity definition page, such as [[Logseq/Entity/Game]], [[Logseq/Entity/Software/Project]], or [[Logseq/Entity/Question]].
	- A page that models more than one entity lists each marker, comma-separated, primary entity first. [[Logseq/Entity]] describes multiple entity membership.

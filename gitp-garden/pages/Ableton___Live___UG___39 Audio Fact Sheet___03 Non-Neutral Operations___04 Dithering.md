- # [39.3.4 Dithering](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/audio-fact-sheet/#dithering)
	- Dither when rendering to a lower bit depth. Dither is low-level noise, so it is non-neutral.
	- Any gain change in Live produces 32-bit audio. Apply dither only once; unless you master in Live, render at 32 bits and skip dither.

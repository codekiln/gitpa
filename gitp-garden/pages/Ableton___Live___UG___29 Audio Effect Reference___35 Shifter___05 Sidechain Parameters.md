- # [29.35.5 Sidechain Parameters](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-audio-effect-reference/#sidechain-parameters-6)
	- ![Shifter’s Sidechain Parameters](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/ShifterSidechainParametersL12.png?auto=&w=320)
	- The triangle at the left of the title bar unfolds the sidechain.
	- **Pitch Mode**: **Internal** uses Coarse and Fine; **MIDI** uses incoming notes from a chosen source.
	- In MIDI mode: **Glide** (ms between notes) and **PB** (pitch bend range, 0–24 semitones).

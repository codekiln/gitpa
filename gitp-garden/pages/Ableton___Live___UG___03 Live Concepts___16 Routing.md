- # [3.16 Routing](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-concepts/#routing)
	- The mixer's In/Out section, shown from View → Mixer Controls, is Live's patchbay: a source and a destination chooser for every track.
	- Tracks can send to audio and MIDI interfaces, to other programs, or to a group track that acts as a submixer. They can take input from outside Live or from another track or device, with monitoring controls deciding when the input is heard.
	- External Audio Effect and External Instrument route signals to hardware from within a device chain.

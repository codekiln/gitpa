- # [36.13 Step Sequencing Automation](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-2/#step-sequencing-automation)
	- In both step sequencers, holding steps lets you edit settings for just those steps:
		- in Clip mode — note settings;
		- in Device or Mix mode — device or mixer automation for the time those steps cover, even on steps without notes.

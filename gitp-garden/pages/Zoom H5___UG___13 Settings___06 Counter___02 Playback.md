- # Set how time is shown during playback
	- During playback, the counter can show the elapsed playback time (count up) or the playback time remaining (count down).
	- 1. Press **MENU**, then use the scroll button to select `PLAY > COUNTER SETUP`.
	- 2. Select `COUNT UP` or `COUNT DOWN` and press the scroll button.

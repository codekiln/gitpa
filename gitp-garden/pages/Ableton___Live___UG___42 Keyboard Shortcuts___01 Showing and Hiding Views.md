- # [42.1 Showing and Hiding Views](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-keyboard-shortcuts/#showing-and-hiding-views)
	- Toggle Full Screen Mode — `Ctrl+Cmd+F` (Win `F11`)
	- Toggle Second Window — `Cmd+Shift+W` (Win `Ctrl+Shift+W`)
	- Toggle Session/Arrangement View — `Tab`
	- Toggle Between Device/Clip View — `Shift+Tab` or `F12`
	- Toggle Device and Clip View — `Option` click view toggle (Win `Alt` click view toggle)
	- Toggle Hot-Swap Mode — `Q`
	- Toggle Drum Rack/Last Selected Pad — `D`
	- Hide/Show Info View — `Shift+?`
	- Hide/Show Video Window — `Cmd+Option+V` (Win `Ctrl+Alt+V`)
	- Hide/Show Browser — `Cmd+Option+B` or `Cmd+Option+5` (Win `Ctrl+Alt+B` or `Ctrl+Alt+5`)
	- Hide/Show Overview — `Cmd+Option+O` (Win `Ctrl+Alt+O`)
	- Hide/Show In/Out — `Cmd+Option+I` (Win `Ctrl+Alt+I`)
	- Hide/Show Sends — `Cmd+Option+S` (Win `Ctrl+Alt+S`)
	- Hide/Show Mixer — `Cmd+Option+M` (Win `Ctrl+Alt+M`)
	- Hide/Show Clip View — `Cmd+Option+3` (Win `Ctrl+Alt+3`)
	- Hide/Show Device View — `Cmd+Option+4` (Win `Ctrl+Alt+4`)
	- Hide/Show the Groove Pool — `Cmd+Option+6` (Win `Ctrl+Alt+6`)
	- Hide/Show the Learn View — `Cmd+Option+7` (Win `Ctrl+Alt+7`)
	- Open the Settings — `Cmd+,` (Win `Ctrl+,`)
	- Close Window/Dialog — `Esc`

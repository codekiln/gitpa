- # [36.11 Track Control And Mixing](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-2/#track-control-and-mixing)
	- **Mix** toggles between Track Mix and Global Mix modes.
	- **Track Mix**: lower buttons pick the track; the first two upper buttons switch between Mix (volume, pan, sends) and Input & Output (routing and monitoring). With more than six returns, arrows scroll the sends.
		- ![The mix controls in Track Mix mode](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2TrackMix.png?auto=&w=320)
	- **Global Mix**: the encoders control volume, pan or sends for the eight visible tracks, chosen with the upper buttons. Arrows scroll extra sends or tracks.
		- ![Controlling the pans of eight tracks](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2Pans.png?auto=&w=320)
	- `Shift` gives fine adjustment. **Master** selects the Main track; press again to go back.
	- With Split Stereo Pan, Global Mix shows the pan value but disables the dial; Track Mix shows the split sliders.

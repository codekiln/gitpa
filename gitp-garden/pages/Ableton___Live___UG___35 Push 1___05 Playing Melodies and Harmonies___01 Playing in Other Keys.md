- # [35.5.1 Playing in Other Keys](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-1/#playing-in-other-keys)
	- **Scales** changes key and scale with the display and the Selection and State Control buttons. The first encoder or the two far-left buttons pick the scale type.
		- ![Key and Scale Selection](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/PushScalesSelection.png)
	- **Fixed Y/N** — with Fixed on, the grid stays put when the key changes and the bottom-left pad stays C, or the nearest key note. With it off, the bottom-left pad is always the root.
	- **In Key/Chromatic** — In Key folds the grid to scale notes only; Chromatic shows every note with in-key notes lit.
	- `Shift` in Scales offers more layouts: "4th" and "3rd" set the interval between rows, ^ and > set whether it runs up or right, and "Sequent" lays notes out in order with no duplicates for a wide range.
	- Key, scale, In Key/Chromatic and Fixed are saved with the Set.
	- Drum recording options (fixed length, Repeat, quantize) work here too. Hold Delete and tap a pad to remove every note of that pitch in the loop.

- # [15.3.1 Bypass Tuning](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-tuning-systems/#bypass-tuning)
	- **Bypass Tuning** makes one MIDI track ignore the tuning; its clips show 12TET notes in the piano roll.
		- ![The Bypass Tuning toggle](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/BypassTuningToggle.png?auto=&w=320)
	- Tracks containing Drum Racks bypass the tuning automatically.

- # [36.4.3 Arrangement Recording](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-2/#arrangement-recording)
	- With Arrangement View in focus, Record toggles Arrangement recording, capturing everything you do on Push 2.
	- With Session View in focus, `Shift`+Record starts Arrangement recording; with the Arrangement in focus, `Shift`+Record toggles Session recording instead.

- # [31.3.3 Filter Section](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#filter-section)
	- A switchable low-pass filter with key tracking and resonance, a high-pass filter, and two modulation slots.
		- ![Drift's Filter Section](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/DriftFilterL12.png)
	- **Freq** — low-pass cutoff. **Type** — Type I, 12 dB/octave DFM-1 that feeds distortion back internally, from gentle sweeps to warm drive; or Type II, 24 dB/octave Cytomic MS2, a Sallen-Key design with soft clipping that limits resonance.
	- **Key** — 0.00 ignores note pitch; 1.00 makes cutoff follow it.
	- **Res** — low-pass resonance. **HP** — high-pass cutoff.
	- Clicking the section shows an X-Y view: the left dot sets HP, the right dot sets LP cutoff horizontally and resonance vertically.
	- **Freq Mod** — two sources for LP cutoff, each -100% to 100%.

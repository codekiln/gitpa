- # Introduction
	- ## Swappable stereo mics
		- The H5 comes with an XY mic that records a stereo image with depth. Swap it for another mic capsule to suit the situation, much like changing lenses on an SLR camera. See [[Zoom H5/UG/04 Mics]].
	- ## Up to 4 tracks at once
		- Besides the swappable stereo mic on the L/R inputs, the main unit has two XLR/TRS inputs, Inputs 1/2. See [[Zoom H5/UG/05 Inputs]].
		- Together they record up to 4 tracks at once: for example ambience, narration, the overall sound and the voices of several performers.
	- ## Recording features
		- The XY mic sits in a shock mount that reduces vibration noise. It handles sound pressure up to 140 dB SPL, louder than earlier recorders could capture, and records stereo with excellent separation.
		- With the L/R inputs, the H5 can record a backup file at the same time, 12 dB lower than the regular recording. Use the backup if an unexpected loud noise distorts the regular recording. See [[Zoom H5/UG/08 Recording/12 Backup Rec]].
		- Every input has its own knob for quick gain adjustment.
	- ## Operation features
		- The H5 records to SDHC cards up to 32 GB.
		- A line output jack sits alongside the headphone output, so the H5 can feed a video camera or other device while you monitor on headphones.
		- Over USB, the H5 works as a card reader, a 2-in/2-out audio interface, or a 4-in/2-out audio interface. Windows needs a driver for 4-in/2-out. See [[Zoom H5/UG/11 USB]].
		- Like other H series recorders, it has a tuner, a metronome, and playback speed and pitch controls. A wired remote control is also available.

- # [31.12.4 Sound Design Tips](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#sound-design-tips-1)
	- Tension's parts affect each other, so it is easy to find settings that make no sound, or very loud ones; watch levels.
	- Think of the sections as one physical object: a slow bow can start an undamped string, but a heavy damper needs a faster bow.
	- The presets show it can do much more than strings.

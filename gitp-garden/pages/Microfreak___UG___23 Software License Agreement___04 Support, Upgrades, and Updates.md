- # 4. Support, Upgrades, and Updates after Product Registration
	- Support, upgrades, and updates are available only after personal product registration. Support covers the current version and the previous version for one year after a new version is published. Arturia may change the form or extent of support, upgrades, and updates at any time.
	- Registration can occur during activation or later over the internet. It requires agreement to the storage and use of the Licensee’s name, address, contact details, email address, and license data for the purposes described in the agreement. Arturia may share these data with engaged third parties, especially distributors, for support and to verify upgrade or update eligibility.

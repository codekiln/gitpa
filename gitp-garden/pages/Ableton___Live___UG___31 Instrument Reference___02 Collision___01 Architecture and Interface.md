- # [31.2.1 Architecture and Interface](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#architecture-and-interface-1)
	- Two exciters, **Mallet** and **Noise**, feed two stereo resonators that can be independent or linked. The exciters start the sound; the resonators shape most of its character. With both exciters off there is no sound.
	- The interface has Mallet and Noise sections; Resonator 1 and Resonator 2 tabs; an LFO tab with two LFOs; a MIDI/MPE tab for pitch bend, mod wheel, aftertouch and their MPE versions; and global settings for voices, retrigger, resonator structure and volume.
	- Turning off unused sections saves CPU.

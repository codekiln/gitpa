- # 9.2.2 Assigning to steps
	- Hold a note in the Play Area and press a step, or hold a step and press notes in the Play Area. Each step can hold up to eight notes.
	- Hold a step to preview it. Assigned notes light red in the Play Area.
	- Hold a Play Area note briefly to see its assigned steps light red. If you hold a chord, only steps containing every held note light red.

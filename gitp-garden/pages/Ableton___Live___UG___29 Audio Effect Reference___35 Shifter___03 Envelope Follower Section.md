- # [29.35.3 Envelope Follower Section](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-audio-effect-reference/#envelope-follower-section)
	- ![Shifter’s Envelope Follower](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/ShifterEnvelopeSectionL12.png?auto=&w=320)

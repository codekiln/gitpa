- # [29.36.4 Spectrogram](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-audio-effect-reference/#spectrogram)
	- ![Spectral Resonator’s Display with Integrated Controls](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/SpectralResonatorDisplayL12.png?auto=&w=320)
	- The spectrogram shows dry (yellow) and wet (blue) frequencies over time, and can be hidden with the toggle at its top left.
	- **Harmonics** — number of resonant harmonics; more is brighter and uses more CPU. In Poly they are shared among the voices.
	- **Quantize** — snaps harmonics to the active scale or tuning, or to chromatic pitches if neither is active

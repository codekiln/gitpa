- # [31.8.10 Mix Section](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#mix-section)
	- Per engine: **Volume**, **Pan** and **Tone Filter**, which cuts lows at positive values and highs at negative values.
		- ![Mix Controls for Each Engine](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/MeldMixSectionL12.png)
	- **Limit** — a per-voice limiter after both engines are mixed and Drive is applied.

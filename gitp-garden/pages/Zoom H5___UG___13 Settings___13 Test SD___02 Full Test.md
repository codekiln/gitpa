- # Conducting a full test
	- 1. Press **MENU**, then use the scroll button to select `SD CARD > PERFORMANCE TEST > FULL TEST`.
	- 2. When the estimated test time appears, select `YES` and press the scroll button.
		- Full test confirmation, progress and result
			- ![01 Full test screens](https://s3.us-east-005.backblazeb2.com/logseq-encode-garden/Zoom%20H5___UG___13%20Settings___13%20Test%20SD___02%20Full%20Test___Asset___01-Full-test-screens.png)
		- While the test runs, the screen shows its progress and the `AVERAGE` and `MAX` access rates.
		- When the test completes, a `MAX` access rate of 100% means the card failed the test (`NG`).
	- 3. To cancel the test, press **MENU**.
		- > [[Note/Info]] Press **PLAY/PAUSE** to pause and resume the test.
	- > [[Note/Info]] A result of `OK` does not guarantee that write errors will never occur. Treat the test as a guide only.

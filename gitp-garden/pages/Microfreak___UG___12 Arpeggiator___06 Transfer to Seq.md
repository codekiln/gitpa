- # 12.6 Transferring an arpeggio to the Sequencer
	- To transfer an arpeggio to a sequence pattern:
		- Activate the Arpeggiator with **Arp | Seq** and play until you find an arpeggio you like. This also works in Paraphonic mode.
		- Press **Hold** and adjust Spice & Dice if desired.
		- Press **Shift + Up | A** or **Order | B** to transfer it to sequence pattern A or B. Playback switches to the Sequencer.
	- The transferred pattern extends to the current sequence length. A three-note arpeggio transferred to an eight-step pattern becomes an eight-step sequence.
	- Spice & Dice effects transfer with the arpeggio, providing those effects in a sequence. Recording in Sequencer mode disables Spice & Dice.
	- Chords created in Chord mode also transfer to the sequence.

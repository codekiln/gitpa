- # 3. Activation of the Software
	- Arturia may require software activation and OEM software registration to control licenses and protect against unlawful copying. The software will not work if the Licensee rejects the agreement.
	- In that case, the product, including the software, may be returned only within 30 days of acquisition. A claim under [[Microfreak/UG/23 Software License Agreement/11 Consequential Damages]] does not apply upon return.

- # [29.26.3 Sidechain Parameters](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-audio-effect-reference/#sidechain-parameters-4)
	- ![The Multiband Dynamics Device With Sidechain Section](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/MultibandDynamicsSidechainL12.png?auto=&w=320)
	- ![button in its title bar](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Device_fold1.png?auto=&w=24)
	- The title bar button unfolds the sidechain.
	- **Sidechain** picks any internal routing point as the trigger. **Gain** sets trigger level only (never heard); **Dry/Wet** mixes sidechain and own signal as the trigger.
	- The headphones button plays only the sidechain signal.

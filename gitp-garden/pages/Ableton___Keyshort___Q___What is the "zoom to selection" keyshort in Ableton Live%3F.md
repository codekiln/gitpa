- # What is the "zoom to selection" keyshort in [[Ableton/Live]]?
	- ## [[AI Answer]]
		- [[Answer/Official]] from [Live 12 Keyboard Shortcuts](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-keyboard-shortcuts/)
		- **Short answer:** `Z` on both macOS and Windows. `X` zooms back out.
		- **Where it applies:**
			- Arrangement View: `Z` is "Zoom to Arrangement Time Selection" and `X` is "Zoom Back from Arrangement Time Selection".
			- Clip View editor: `Z` is "Zoom to Clip Selection" and `X` is "Zoom Back from Clip Selection".
		- **Latching:** the manual says `Z` toggles zooming into the Arrangement selection when you hold it, so holding `Z` zooms in and releasing it zooms back out. Tapping `Z` then `X` is the persistent version. The manual notes the momentary latching can be turned off with an `Options.txt` entry.
		- **Computer MIDI Keyboard:** when it is turned on, `Z` and `X` lower and raise its octave instead of zooming.

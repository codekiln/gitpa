- # 12.2 Gates and Triggers revisited
	- Pressing a key produces a gate that stays high while the key is held. The Arpeggiator and Sequencer also generate gates.
	- With **Amp | Mod** off, the Arpeggiator or Sequencer gates control the amplifier directly. With it on, those gates trigger the Envelope, which controls the amplifier.
	- Set gate length in **Utility > Preset > Default gate length**. To hear its effect on the Arpeggiator, turn **Amp | Mod** off, hold a chord, and adjust the setting.

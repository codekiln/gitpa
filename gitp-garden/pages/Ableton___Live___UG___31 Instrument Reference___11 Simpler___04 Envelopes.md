- # [31.11.4 Envelopes](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#envelopes-2)
	- Three [[ADSR]] envelopes, for amplitude, filter and pitch, chosen with buttons and edited with knobs or handles. Filter and pitch have amount controls at the top right.
		- ![Simpler's Filter and Amplitude Envelope Controls](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/SimplerEnvelopesL12.png)
	- The amplitude envelope can loop: Loop and Trigger restart from the start if the note outlasts Decay, taking **Time** to return; Beat and Sync restart after **Rate**.
		- ![The Amplitude Envelope Loop Mode Chooser and Time Control](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/SimplerAmpEnvLoopChooserL12.png)

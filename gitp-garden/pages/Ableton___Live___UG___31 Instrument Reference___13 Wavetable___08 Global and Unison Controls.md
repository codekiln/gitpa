- # [31.13.8 Global and Unison Controls](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#global-and-unison-controls)
	- ![Wavetable's Global Controls](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/WavetableGlobalControlsL12.png)
	- **Transpose**, **Volume** (multiplicative).
	- **Poly/Mono** — Mono is one voice with legato envelopes and **Glide**; Poly uses **Poly Voices**.
	- **Unison** modes:
		- **Classic** — evenly detuned, alternating left and right.
		- **Shimmer** — randomly jittered pitch for a reverb-like shimmer, plus slight wavetable offset.
		- **Noise** — faster jitter for breathy, noisy textures.
		- **Phase Sync** — Classic detune with phases synced at note start, for a phaser-like sweep.
		- **Position spread** — wavetable positions spread across voices, plus slight detune.
		- **Random note** — random position and detune per voice at each note.
	- **Voices** — oscillators per wavetable oscillator; more is thicker, fewer clearer. **Amount** — unison depth; multiplicative.

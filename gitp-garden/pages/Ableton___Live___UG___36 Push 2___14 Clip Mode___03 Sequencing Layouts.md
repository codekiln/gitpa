- # [36.14.3 Sequencing Layouts](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-2/#sequencing-layouts)
	- In the step sequencer layouts, a translucent white box marks the sequenceable area — one page of steps. A box at the far left shows its pitch range.
		- ![Clip mode's sequenceable area](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2MIDIClipSequenceableArea.png?auto=&w=320)
	- Lines at the far left show where the clip has notes, thicker where there are more, so notes can be found without the computer screen.
	- ## 36.14.3.1 Melodic Sequencer
		- Eight notes per page. Move the area sideways with the loop length pads or Page Left/Right, and up or down with the Octave buttons or touch strip.
	- ## 36.14.3.2 Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes
		- The display fits every note in the clip and highlights where selected notes sit. Move sideways with Page Left/Right or the loop length pads (fifth row while holding Layout).
		- In the drum and slicing Loop Selector and 16 Velocities layouts, only the selected pad shows; move sideways with the loop length pads or Page Left/Right, and pick a different pad to change rows.

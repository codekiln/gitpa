- # [36.3.6 Real-time Recording](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-2/#real-time-recording)
	- Optionally turn on **Metronome** (`Shift` + Master encoder sets its volume; lit buttons pulse when on), then press **Record**.
		- ![The Record button](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2Record.png?auto=&w=185)
	- A count-in, if set in Live, shows as a bar flashing across the display.
	- Pads you play are recorded. Record again stops recording but keeps playing; a third press overdubs; further presses toggle between playback and overdub. Progress bars show each playing clip's position.
	- **Accent** makes every played or step-sequenced note full velocity (127), overriding velocity sensitivity and the 16 Velocities pads. Tap to latch it; hold for momentary use.
	- **New** stops the selected clip and prepares a new one on the track for practising. By default it also copies clips playing on other tracks to a new scene and keeps them playing; the Setup menu's Workflow setting changes this.
		- ![The New button](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2New.png?auto=&w=191)

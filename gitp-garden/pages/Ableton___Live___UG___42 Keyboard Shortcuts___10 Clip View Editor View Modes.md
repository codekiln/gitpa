- # [42.10 Clip View Editor View Modes](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-keyboard-shortcuts/#clip-view-editor-view-modes)
	- Switch Between Sample/Envelopes Tabs — `Option+Tab` (Win `Ctrl+Tab`)
	- Switch Between Notes/Envelopes/MPE Tabs — `Option+Tab` (Win `Ctrl+Tab`)
	- Switch to Sample/Notes Tab — `Option+Shift+1` (Win `Alt+Shift+1`)
	- Switch to Envelopes Tab — `Option+Shift+2` (Win `Alt+Shift+2`)
	- Switch to MPE Tab — `Option+Shift+3` (Win `Alt+Shift+3`)

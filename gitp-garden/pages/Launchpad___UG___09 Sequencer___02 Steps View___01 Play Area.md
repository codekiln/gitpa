- # 9.2.1 Using the Play Area
	- On Chromatic and Scale tracks, Up and Down shift the Play Area by an octave.
	- On Drum tracks, Up and Down shift by 16 notes (one 4 × 4 grid). Left and Right move the Drum layout by one row of four notes.

- # Turning the power on and off
	- ## Turning the power on
		- 1. Slide **POWER/HOLD** toward the power symbol (⏻), away from HOLD.
			- The display shows `Now Loading...` while the H5 starts.
		- > [[Note/Info]] The first time the power is turned on after purchase, set the date and time: [[Zoom H5/UG/07 Prep/04 Date and Time]].
		- > [[Note/Info]] If `No SD Card!` appears, check that the SD card is inserted properly.
		- > [[Note/Info]] If `Card Protected!` appears, the card's write protection is on. Slide the lock switch on the card to turn it off.
		- > [[Note/Info]] If `Invalid SD Card!` appears, the card is not formatted correctly. Format it or use a different card. See [[Zoom H5/UG/13 Settings/12 Format SD]].
	- ## Turning the power off
		- 1. Slide **POWER/HOLD** toward the power symbol (⏻).
		- > [[Note/Info]] Keep holding the switch there until `Goodbye See You!` appears.
	- ## Using the hold function
		- The hold function disables the buttons to prevent accidental operation during recording.
		- ### Activating the hold function
			- 1. Slide **POWER/HOLD** to HOLD.
				- The display shows `Hold`.
			- > [[Note/Info]] A remote control still works while hold is on. See [[Zoom H5/UG/14 Other Functions/03 Remote Control]].
		- ### Deactivating the hold function
			- 1. Slide **POWER/HOLD** back to the center.

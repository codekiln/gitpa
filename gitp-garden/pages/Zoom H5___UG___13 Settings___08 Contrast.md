- # Adjusting the display contrast
	- 1. Press **MENU**, then use the scroll button to select `SYSTEM > DISPLAY CONTRAST`.
	- 2. Use the scroll button to adjust the contrast, then press it.
		- Display contrast screen
			- ![01 Display contrast screen](https://s3.us-east-005.backblazeb2.com/logseq-encode-garden/Zoom%20H5___UG___13%20Settings___08%20Contrast___Asset___01-Display-contrast-screen.png)
		- The contrast ranges from 1 to 10.

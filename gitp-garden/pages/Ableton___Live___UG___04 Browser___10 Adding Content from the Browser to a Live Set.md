- # [4.10 Adding Content from the Browser to a Live Set](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/working-with-the-browser/#adding-content-from-the-browser-to-a-live-set)
	- Drag items onto Session or Arrangement tracks; dropping them right of the Session tracks or below the Arrangement tracks creates a new track.
	- In Session View, double-click or `Enter` loads a device onto the selected track; a sample goes into a Simpler on a MIDI track or into a clip slot on an audio track. In Arrangement View, the same loads the item onto the selected track.
	- Files can also be dropped in from Finder or Explorer.

- # [36.3.7 Fixed Length Recording](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-2/#fixed-length-recording)
	- **Fixed Length** makes new clips a set length; hold it to choose the length. With it off, recording continues until Record, New or Play/Stop.
		- ![Fixed Length recording options](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2FixedLengthMenu.png?auto=&w=320)
	- Recording creates an empty clip of that length and starts at its beginning, following global launch quantization. With **Phrase Sync**, it starts at the matching point in the phrase: with 4 bars starting at bar 7, recording begins at bar 3 of the new clip.
	- Turning Fixed Length on mid-recording stops recording and loops the last bars of the clip.

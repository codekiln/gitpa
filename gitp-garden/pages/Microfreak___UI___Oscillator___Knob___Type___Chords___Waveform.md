- # Waveform (Chords)
	- Set by the [[Microfreak/UI/Oscillator/Knob/Shape]] while the oscillator model is [[Microfreak/UI/Oscillator/Knob/Type/Chords]].
	- Sets the waveform. The first half of the knob goes through a selection of string-machine-like raw waveforms, different combinations of the organ and string drawbars; the second half scans a small wavetable of 16 waveforms.

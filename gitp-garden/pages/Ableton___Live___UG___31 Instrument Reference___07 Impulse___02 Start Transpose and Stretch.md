- # [31.7.2 Start, Transpose and Stretch](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#start-transpose-and-stretch)
	- **Start** — offset into the sample, up to 100 ms.
	- **Transp** — ±48 semitones, modulated by velocity or random.
	- **Stretch** — -100% to 100%; negative shortens, positive lengthens. Mode A suits low sounds like toms and bass; Mode B suits high ones like cymbals. Modulated by velocity.

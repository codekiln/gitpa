- # [29.9.1 Sidechain Parameters](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-audio-effect-reference/#sidechain-parameters-1)
	- ![The Compressor Device With Sidechain Section](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/CompressorSidechainL12.png?auto=&w=320)
	- ![button in its title bar](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Device_fold1.png?auto=&w=24)
	- The title bar button unfolds the sidechain controls. A sidechain lets another signal, or one frequency band, decide when to compress.
	- Left side, external:
		- **Sidechain** on — choose any internal routing point as the trigger
		- **Gain** — trigger level only; the sidechain is never heard
		- **Dry/Wet** — 100% triggers only from the sidechain, 0% ignores it
		- Automatic Makeup is unavailable with an external sidechain.
	- Right side, sidechain EQ — triggers from one frequency band, of this track or of the external source.
	- The headphones button between them plays only the sidechain signal, to hear what drives the compressor.

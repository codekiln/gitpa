- # [31.13.3 Sub Oscillator](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#sub-oscillator)
	- **Sub** on/off with **Gain**. **Tone** — 0% is a pure sine; higher adds harmonics. **Octave** — shift down one or two octaves from the played note.

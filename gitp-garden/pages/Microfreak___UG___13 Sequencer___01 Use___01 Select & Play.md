- # 13.1.1. Selecting and Playing a Sequence Pattern
	- A new preset has empty patterns. A factory or saved preset may contain data in A or B. Press A or B to load that pattern; the display shows “Sequence X Loaded.”
	- During playback, press A or B to switch patterns. Both patterns have the same length within a preset and play one at a time. Recorded patterns are saved with their preset.
	- To discard recent changes and reload the last saved version of a pattern, hold Shift and press its A or B icon.
	- To erase a pattern, hold its A or B icon and press Hold. Both buttons blink until the erase finishes.
	- > [[Note/Info]] You can erase or reload A while B plays, and the reverse.
	- Once a pattern contains notes, pressing a keyboard key starts the currently selected pattern; you need not press Play first.
	- A pattern can hold both notes and modulation data.

- # [33.3.3 MIDI Monitor](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/max-for-live-devices/#midi-monitor)
	- ![The MIDI Monitor Effect](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/MaxMIDIMonitorL12.png?auto=&w=320)
	- A utility that shows incoming MIDI and MPE data.
	- **Note** display — incoming notes on a keyboard, with root, chord name and velocity
	- **Flow** diagram — a running list of notes and other messages such as pitch bend and aftertouch; **Freeze** pauses it and **Clear** empties it
		- ![The Flow Diagram](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/MaxMIDIMonitorFlowL12.png?auto=&w=320)
	- **MPE** display — a running view of note, velocity, slide, pressure and per-note pitch
		- ![The MPE Display](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/MaxMIDIMonitorMPEL12.png?auto=&w=320)

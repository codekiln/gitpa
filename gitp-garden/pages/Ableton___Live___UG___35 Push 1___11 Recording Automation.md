- # [35.11 Recording Automation](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-1/#recording-automation)
	- **Automation** toggles Live's Session Automation Arm, so encoder moves record into the clip. Press again to stop.
	- Hold Delete and touch an encoder to clear that parameter's automation, or reset it to default if it has none.
	- The display marks automated parameters with a rectangle and shows overridden values in brackets. `Shift` + Automation re-enables overridden automation.

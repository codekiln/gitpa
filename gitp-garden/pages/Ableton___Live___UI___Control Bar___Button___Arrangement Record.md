- # Arrangement Record button
	- Records into the Arrangement: armed tracks record their input as new clips, and Session clips launched, their property changes, and mixer and device automation are written to the Arrangement.
	- Press it again or stop playback to finish recording.
	- With Start Playback with Record on in the Record, Warp & Launch Settings, recording starts when the button is pressed; otherwise it waits for Play or a launched Session clip. Shift-click does the opposite.
	- **Behavior** — toggle.
	- **Remote control** — MIDI or key mapping. [[Launchpad/Custom/Mode/1 Arrangement]] maps a Launchpad pad to it.

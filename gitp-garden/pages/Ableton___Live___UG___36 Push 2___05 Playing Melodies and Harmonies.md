- # [36.5 Playing Melodies and Harmonies](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-2/#playing-melodies-and-harmonies)
	- Switch tracks with the lower display buttons or the arrow keys, or press **Add Track** to choose MIDI, Audio or Return and optionally load a device. Inside a group, new tracks join the group.
		- ![Choosing a track type and optionally loading a device](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2AddTrackBrowser.png?auto=&w=320)
	- `Shift` + a track's lower display button, then a pad in the outer ring, sets the track colour.
	- On an instrument track the grid plays notes in C major by default: bottom left is C1 (Octave buttons shift it), each row up is a fourth higher, and each pad right is the next scale note.
		- ![C major scale](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2CMajor.png?auto=&w=320)
	- Pad colours: track colour for the root, white for other scale notes, green for notes playing (every pad with that note), red while recording.
	- A triad is the same shape anywhere on the grid.
		- ![C major chord](https://ableton-production.imgix.net/live-manual/12/Push2CMajorChord.png?auto=&w=320)
	- Hold Layout for the loop length controls; `Shift`+Layout locks them; Layout unlocks.

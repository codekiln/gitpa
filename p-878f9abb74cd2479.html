Microfreak/UG/22 Declaration of Conformity

  • 22. Declaration of Conformity

      • 22.1 FCC

        • Note/Warning Unapproved modifications can void the user's authority to operate the equipment.

        • The unit meets FCC Part 15 requirements. Use is subject to two conditions:
          • The unit must not cause harmful interference.
          • The unit must accept interference it receives, including interference that may cause undesired operation.

        • Responsible party in the United States

          • Zedra, 185 Alewife Brook Parkway, #210, Cambridge, MA 02138
          • Telephone: +1 857 285 5953
        • Trade name: ARTURIA
        • Model: MicroFreak
        • Testing found the unit within the FCC limits for a Class B digital device. Those limits aim to provide reasonable protection from harmful interference in residential installations.
        • If the unit interferes with radio or television reception, try these measures:
          • Reorient or relocate the receiving antenna.
          • Increase the distance between the unit and receiver.
          • Connect the unit to an outlet on a different circuit from the receiver.
          • Ask the dealer or an experienced radio/TV technician for help.

      • 22.2 Canada

        • The Class B digital apparatus complies with Canadian ICES-003.
        • The French statement declares that Class B digital apparatus complies with Canadian standard NMB-003.

      • 22.3 CE

        • Testing found the unit within the European Council limits for electromagnetic compatibility under Directive 2014/30/EU and low voltage under Directive 2014/35/EU.

      • 22.4 UKCA

        • The unit meets the essential electromagnetic compatibility requirements of the UK Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2016.

      • 22.5 ROHS

        • The unit uses lead-free solder and meets the requirements of the RoHS Directive 2011/65/EU.

      • 22.6 WEEE

        • The crossed-out wheeled-bin symbol
        • This symbol indicates that the electrical and electronic equipment belongs at a collection point for treatment, recovery, and recycling, rather than in household waste. Follow national law and the WEEE Directive 2012/19/EU. For collection information, contact the local municipal office, household waste service, or the shop where the product was purchased.

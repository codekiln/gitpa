Launchpad/UG/05 Session Mode/03 Clip Functions/04 Double Clip Length


  • 5.3.4 Double a Clip’s Length

    • Double expands a clip to twice its length, such as a two-bar loop becoming four bars.
    • In Session Mode, hold Shift + Duplicate and press the clip pad. For a selected playing clip in Note or Device Mode, hold Shift and press Duplicate once. In Note Mode, the Double control extends the selected clip for a longer loop or overdub.

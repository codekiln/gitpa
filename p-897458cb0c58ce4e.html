Microfreak/UG/02 Installation


  • 02 Installation

      • 02.01 Usage Precautions

        • Use only the power adapter supplied by Arturia. Arturia is not responsible for damage caused by an unauthorized power supply.
        • The touch-capacitive keyboard can work with a power bank, but the MicroFreak must be grounded to function fully. Arturia recommends the supplied three-pin wall plug.
        • After electrostatic discharge interference, the headphone output may go silent and the keyboard may malfunction. Restart the instrument to recover.
        • Prolonged radio-frequency interference may disrupt the keyboard. Restart the instrument to recover.

      • 02.02 Warning

        • Keep power cords and connecting cables out of walkways, where they could be stepped on or rolled over.
        • Arturia does not recommend extension cords. If one is necessary, it must handle this product's maximum current; consult a local electrician about power requirements.
        • Use only components supplied or recommended by Arturia, and follow the safety markings and instructions supplied with those components.

      • 02.03 Notice

        • The manufacturer's warranty does not cover service charges caused by not knowing how a correctly functioning feature works. Read this manual and consult your dealer before requesting service.

      • 02.04 Precautions include, but are not limited to, the following:

        • Read and understand all instructions. Follow the instructions printed on the instrument.
        • Before cleaning, disconnect the power plug and USB cable. Use a soft, dry cloth. Avoid gasoline, alcohol, acetone, turpentine, other organic solvents, liquid cleaners, sprays, and overly wet cloths.
        • Keep the instrument away from water and moisture. Place it somewhere stable where it cannot fall.
        • Do not put heavy objects on the instrument or block its ventilation openings. Keep it away from heat vents and places with poor air circulation.
        • Use only the supplied AC adapter, and check that local line voltage matches the adapter's specified input voltage.
        • Do not open the instrument or insert objects into it; this can cause fire or electric shock. Do not spill liquid on it.
        • If the instrument malfunctions, take it to a qualified service center. Opening and removing the cover voids the warranty; improper testing may cause electric shock or other malfunctions.
        • Do not use the instrument during thunderstorms, in hot sunlight, or near a gas leak.
        • Arturia is not responsible for damage or data loss caused by improper operation.
        • Arturia recommends shielded audio cables shorter than 3 meters and ferrite-equipped CV/Gate cables.

      • 02.05 Register your Instrument

        • Registering the MicroFreak establishes legal ownership and provides access to Arturia Technical Support and update notices. Registration also offers an option to receive Arturia news and promotions.
        • Sign in to your Arturia account, open My Registered Products, and add the MicroFreak using the serial number on the sticker underneath the instrument.

      • 02.06 Connecting the MicroFreak to the World

        • Turn off all audio equipment before connecting anything to avoid damage to speakers, the MicroFreak, or other equipment.
        • After making connections, set all levels to zero. Power on the devices with the audio amplifier or monitoring system last, then raise the volume to a comfortable level.

        • Connectors

          • Audio output: 6.35 mm (1/4-inch) TS or balanced TRS output
          • Headphones: 3.5 mm (1/8-inch) TRS jack; mono signal
          • MIDI input and output: 1/8-inch TRS jacks
          • USB: standard USB Type-B
          • Power: DC input, 2.1 mm inner diameter and 5.5 mm outer diameter

        • Note/Info Use the included gray 1/8-inch TRS to 5-pin DIN adapter to connect external MIDI devices.

      • 02.07 Get the Latest Firmware

        • The latest MicroFreak firmware adds features described in Microfreak/UG/01 Welcome and Introduction/02 New in FW 5.0.0. Install the latest firmware to get the full MicroFreak experience.

        • Download the firmware

          • Open the Arturia Downloads and Manuals page.
          • Find MIDI Control Center in the sidebar and install the latest macOS or Windows version.
          • Find MicroFreak in the same sidebar and download its latest firmware.

        • Install the firmware

          • Connect the MicroFreak over USB and power it on, then open MIDI Control Center.
          • Select MicroFreak from the DEVICE menu in the upper-left corner. The menu displays the current firmware revision:
          • Click Firmware Revision to open the dialog showing the current version:
          • Click Upgrade, then use the operating system file dialog to select the downloaded firmware file.

        • Note/Warning While the firmware update is in progress, do not turn the MicroFreak's knobs, power it off, or disconnect its USB cable.

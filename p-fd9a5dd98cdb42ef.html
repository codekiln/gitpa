Microfreak/UI/Oscillator

  • Wave knob

    • One of the three sound-shaping knobs of the digital oscillator; what it changes depends on the oscillator model, and it can be a modulation destination in the Matrix.
    • Turns in Slow or Fast mode: Microfreak/UG/06 Dig Osc/02 Param Controls/01 Knob Speed.

GitP/A/Session/26/10/24-Sat

The Unstable over USB › MIDI › ↗

  • Times below follow the recording. Control names come from Microfreak/UG/21 Appendix D - CC Values. With V.Analog, the panel's Wave knob (CC 10) sets Detune, the Timbre knob (CC 12) sets Shape, and the Shape knob (CC 13) sets Wave. Microfreak/UG/06 Dig Osc/03 Types/06 VAnalog

The Unstable over USB › Chapters › 10:00 Detune and Launchpad pressure › ↗

  • Detune (the Wave knob, CC 10) changes from 10:00 and reaches 49 by 12:00. Launchpad pad pressure, recorded as per-note pressure, arrives from 11:00 and carries through to the end. Oscillator type changes again between 13:14 and 13:19 and returns to its starting value.

Microfreak/Preset/151 The Unstable

Notes › Oscillator › ↗

  • On the panel, V.Analog puts Detune on the Wave knob (CC 10), Shape on the Timbre knob (CC 12) and Wave on the Shape knob (CC 13). In the session codekiln worked this out by ear: "So timbre is the shape."

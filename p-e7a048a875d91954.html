logseq-entity
Logseq/Entity/Definition

  • Book Section Level 1

    • In this garden, Book Section Level 1 pages model the topmost numbered parts of a book or manual.
    • A Level 1 page sits directly under the work's root page. Its two-digit prefix follows the source's chapter order; its H1 retains the chapter number and full heading.
    • It contains the chapter's introductory material and embeds its Level 2 children in reading order. An empty chapter shell is unnecessary when the chapter has not been imported.
    • The shared section shape lives on Logseq/Entity/Book/Section. Microfreak/UG/06 Dig Osc is an example.

Logseq/Entity/Book/Section

Book Section › What counts › ↗

  • Logseq/Entity/Book/Section/Level 1, Logseq/Entity/Book/Section/Level 2, Logseq/Entity/Book/Section/Level 3, and Logseq/Entity/Book/Section/Level 4 specify the depth. The instance declares its most specific level in logseq-entity::; the level page points here for the shared shape.

Book Section › Page shape › Only embed L1 -> L2 -> L3 › Example/Good › ↗

  • A top-level Logseq/Entity/Book entity or a parent page of a Level 1 page has links to Logseq/Entity/Book/Section/Level 1 sections, not embeds.

Book Section › Page shape › Only embed L1 -> L2 -> L3 › Example/Good › ↗

  • Logseq/Entity/Book/Section/Level 1 has an embed of Logseq/Entity/Book/Section/Level 2, and Logseq/Entity/Book/Section/Level 2 has an embed of Logseq/Entity/Book/Section/Level 3. Beneath that, it uses links to Logseq/Entity/Book/Section/Level 4.

Logseq/Entity/Book/Section/Level 1

  • Book Section Level 1

    • In this garden, Book Section Level 1 pages model the topmost numbered parts of a book or manual.
    • A Level 1 page sits directly under the work's root page. Its two-digit prefix follows the source's chapter order; its H1 retains the chapter number and full heading.
    • It contains the chapter's introductory material and embeds its Level 2 children in reading order. An empty chapter shell is unnecessary when the chapter has not been imported.
    • The shared section shape lives on Logseq/Entity/Book/Section. Microfreak/UG/06 Dig Osc is an example.

Logseq/Entity/Definition

  • Book Section Level 3

    • In this garden, Book Section Level 3 pages model sections directly beneath a Level 2 section.
    • A Level 3 page sits under its Level 2 parent. Its two-digit prefix orders siblings within that parent; its H1 retains the source subsection number and heading when present.
    • It holds its own content and embeds any Level 4 children at their source positions. Microfreak/UG/06 Dig Osc/02 Param Controls/01 Knob Speed is an example.
    • The shared section shape lives on Logseq/Entity/Book/Section.

    • MIDI

      • Download the session MIDI · GitP/A/Session/26/10/26-Mon/Asset/MIDI/Session/mid
      • Times below follow the recording. Control names come from Microfreak/UG/21 Appendix D - CC Values. What the Wave (CC 10), Timbre (CC 12) and Shape (CC 13) knobs do depends on the oscillator: with Noise they change the noise character, with Chords Wave selects the chord, and with V.Analog Wave sets detune. No oscillator-type changes (CC 9) were recorded.

Launchpad/UG/01 Introduction


  • 1. Introduction

    • Launchpad Pro is a grid controller for producing and performing with Ableton Live or external MIDI hardware.
    • Its 64 velocity- and pressure-sensitive RGB pads can launch clips, play notes, and sequence steps. The four-track, 32-step sequencer provides probability and mutation controls for evolving patterns.
    • Eight Custom Modes can send MIDI to software or hardware. The two MIDI output ports extend control to external instruments.
    • The unit can operate without a computer when powered through its USB-C port by the included wall adapter.


      • 1.1 Launchpad Pro Key Features

        • Ableton Live integration: play, record, and produce from the controller.
        • Pads: 64 RGB pads sense velocity and pressure.
        • Sequencer: four tracks with 32-step patterns, scenes, probability, and mutation.
        • Chord Mode: explore, build, save, and play chords from the grid.
        • Note and Scale Modes: play melodies and chords in key; Drum Mode maps a drum rack to the grid.
        • MIDI connections: MIDI In, Out 1, and Out 2/Thru connect external equipment.
        • Custom Modes: eight editable MIDI layouts made with Novation Components.


      • 1.2 In the Box

        • Launchpad Pro
        • USB-C to USB-A cable
        • USB-C to USB-C cable
        • USB-A power adapter
        • Three TRS minijack-to-DIN MIDI adapters

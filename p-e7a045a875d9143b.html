Logseq/Entity/Book/Section/Level 4

  • Book Section Level 4

    • In this garden, Book Section Level 4 pages model distinct headed sections directly beneath a Level 3 section.
    • A Level 4 page sits under its Level 3 parent. Its two-digit prefix orders siblings within that parent, even when the source leaves the heading unnumbered; its H1 retains the source heading and any source number.
    • A Level 4 page holds its own content. Smaller headings remain nested blocks within it unless the source hierarchy genuinely continues beyond this model.
    • The shared section shape lives on Logseq/Entity/Book/Section.

Microfreak/UG/14 Config/01 Utility & MCC/01 Preset

  • 14.1.1 Preset

    • These settings are saved with each preset and are available in Utility. They are unavailable in MCC.

    • Voice

      • Bend Range: 0–24 semitones; up to two octaves.
      • Glide Mode: Time, Sync, or Rate; controls whether the Glide knob sets glide time, tempo-synced glide, or glide rate.
      • Unison Spread: 0.001–12.000; spread between the four voices.
      • Unison Count: 2–4 voices in unison mode.

    • Modulations

      • Envelope Legato: Off or On; controls envelope reset on a keyboard trigger.
      • Envelope Snap: Off or On; sharpens the envelope decay.
      • LFO Retrig: Off, On, or Legato; controls retriggering on keyboard triggers and during legato playing.
      • Press Mode: Aftertouch or Velocity; selects the pressure mode.
      • Key/Arp Mode: Linear or Random; Random generates a new random value for each key pressed.
      • Velo Amp Mod: 0–10; controls how much velocity affects output volume.

    • Preset Volume

      • Preset Volume: −12 to +12; relative preset volume.

    • Scale

      • Scale: Global, Off, Major, Minor, Harmonic Minor, Dorian, Mixolydian, Blues, or Pentatonic.
      • Root Note: C, C♯, D, D♯, E, F, F♯, G, G♯, A, A♯, or B.

    • Seq/Arp

      • Seq Length: 4–64 steps.
      • Default Gate Length: 10–90; default 50, for sequencer and arpeggiator gates.
      • Seq 1–4 Smooth: Off or On for each sequencer modulation track.

    • Vocoder

      • Vocoder Hiss Mode: Off, Switched, or Pass; controls whether vocoder hiss is heard.
      • Vocoder Hiss Volume: −20 to 0; sets the vocoder hiss volume.

  • 03.01 Front panel overview

    • The MicroFreak's front panel is compact.

      • 03.01.01 Top Row

        • Top Row

          • 03.01.01.01 The Matrix

            • The matrix
            • The Modulation Matrix is an electronic patchbay that routes modulation sources to destinations. Turning the white Matrix encoder moves the connection LED between patch points; after the last point, it cycles back to the first. Click the encoder to set the selected connection's modulation amount.
            • Each connection acts like a patch cord with an attenuator for positive or negative modulation. Parameters with knobs can generally be modulation destinations.
            • Sources occupy rows 1–5. Destinations occupy columns 1–7: columns 1–4 are fixed, while columns 5–7 are assignable.

          • 03.01.01.02 Paraphonic

            • When the Paraphonic button is lit, the MicroFreak can trigger four voices independently. The voices share one analog filter but can have different amplitudes. Internal VCA envelopes shape each voice's volume along with the Main Envelope.
            • The Main Envelope, Pressure, Keyboard, and Arpeggiator can produce polyphonic modulation. Routing them to polyphonic destinations such as Oscillator Type, Wave, Timbre, or Shape gives each voice its own modulation.
            • Selecting paraphonic mode

          • 03.01.01.03 Panel Select

            • A loaded preset recalls saved parameter values, which may differ from the panel's current knob positions. Press Panel to apply the current knob positions to the sound, so subsequent edits match what you hear.
            • Panel Select

          • 03.01.01.04 The Display and the Preset encoder

            • The OLED display shows information about knobs and buttons. Turn the Preset encoder beside it to browse presets; the display shows each preset's name and category.
            • Firmware 5.0.0 increased capacity from 384 to 512 slots. A bank of 64 new presets can be loaded from the MIDI Control Center. Empty presets default to the name “Init” and category “Bass.”

            • Note/Info Factory presets are protected against overwrite by default. Change this in Utility > Misc > Mem Protect.

            • The Preset manager

          • 03.01.01.05 Save

            • Press Save to save a preset. Microfreak/UG/04 Presets explains the steps.

            • Note/Info Saving is unavailable in Utility mode. Leave Utility mode before saving a preset.

            • Saving Presets

          • 03.01.01.06 Utility

            • Utility contains global settings and settings for each preset, including Preset Volume, Bend Range, and Pressure mode.
            • Access to the Utility settings

          • 03.01.01.07 Master Volume

            • Master Volume sets the global output level for both the line and headphone outputs. To balance one preset against another, set Utility > Preset > Preset volume.
            • The Volume knob

      • 03.01.02 Middle Row

        • Middle row of the MicroFreak

          • 03.01.02.01 Keyboard glide

            • Glide smooths the pitch transition between notes. Fully counterclockwise, the transition is immediate; turning the knob clockwise increases the glide time. Microfreak/UG/10 Keyboard/03 Glide explains Glide in detail.
            • The Glide knob

          • 03.01.02.02 Digital oscillator

            • The Digital Oscillator generates the MicroFreak's core sound. The Analog Filter, Envelopes, and LFO shape it. Type, Wave, Timbre, and Shape control its parameters; Microfreak/UG/06 Dig Osc explains them in detail.
            • The Digital Oscillator

          • 03.01.02.03 Analog filter

            • The Analog Filter emphasizes or suppresses harmonics in the Digital Oscillator sound, changing its timbre. Cutoff sets where filtering begins; Resonance emphasizes frequencies near that point. Microfreak/UG/07 Filter explains the filter in detail.
            • The Analog Filter
            • The three filter types
              • Low-pass (LPF) attenuates frequencies above cutoff.
              • Band-pass (BPF) attenuates frequencies above and below cutoff.
              • High-pass (HPF) attenuates frequencies below cutoff.

          • 03.01.02.04 Cycling Envelope

            • The Cycling Envelope generates modulation signals for destinations in the Matrix. Unlike a standard envelope, it can retrigger after its last stage finishes. Microfreak/UG/09 Envelope Gen covers its controls.
            • The Cycling Envelope Generator

      • 03.01.03 Bottom Row

        • Bottom row of the MicroFreak

          • 03.01.03.01 Octave select

            • The octave buttons select the active keyboard range.
            • Octave select

          • 03.01.03.02 Shift

            • Hold Shift to access functions printed in blue on the panel and other secondary functions. Microfreak/UG/20 Appendix C - Cheat Sheet lists them.
            • The shift button
            • Shift functions
              • Toggle between Arpeggiator and Sequencer.
              • Set the swing rate.
              • Control the Attack and Fall shapes of the Cycling Envelope.
              • Transpose or reload sequences, or copy an arpeggio to a sequence.

          • 03.01.03.03 ARP/Seq (Arpeggiator/Sequencer)

            • The Arpeggiator plays notes derived from the held keys. Microfreak/UG/12 Arpeggiator and Microfreak/UG/13 Sequencer share several controls.
            • The Arpeggiator and the Sequencers
            • Arp | Seq switches between the Arpeggiator and Sequencer. Oct | Mod sets the Arpeggiator range or selects one of four Sequencer modulation tracks.
            • Rate sets the tempo in BPM. Press the knob to enable Sync; with Sync active, the knob changes the time division relative to the set tempo.

          • 03.01.03.04 LFO

            • The low-frequency oscillator produces modulation at 0.05–100 Hz and offers six waveforms.
            • The LFO
            • Shape selects a waveform
              • Sine rises and falls smoothly.
              • Triangle rises and falls linearly.
              • Rising sawtooth rises linearly and then drops suddenly.
              • Square switches suddenly between minimum and maximum.
              • Random stepped jumps between randomly generated values.
              • Random gliding moves gradually between randomly generated values.
            • Rate sets LFO speed. Press it to enable Sync, which follows the Sequencer/Arpeggiator tempo clock. With Sync off, Rate sets the speed independently.

          • 03.01.03.05 The General Envelope Generator

            • The Envelope Generator shapes a tone’s loudness or timbre and can be patched to destinations in the Matrix. Attack, Decay/Release, and Sustain affect the filter by default; Filter Amt sets the envelope’s effect on the filter. Microfreak/UG/09 Envelope Gen explains the controls in detail.
            • The Envelope Generator
            • With Amp Mod active, the envelope also shapes VCA loudness.

          • 03.01.03.06 Keyboard section

            • The 25-key capacitive keyboard generates gate, pitch, and pressure. The octave buttons extend its two-octave range. The section also includes the Icon strip and the Bend, Spice, and Dice touch controls.

            • Note/Info Utility can make the keyboard generate velocity in place of pressure.

            • The Keyboard
            • Utility or MIDI Control Center settings determine whether the keyboard provides aftertouch or velocity. USB and MIDI out can send fully polyphonic MIDI control to other devices.

          • 03.01.03.07 The Icon strip

            • The eight icons above the keyboard provide Arpeggiator, Pattern Generator, Sequencer, Spice, Dice, and Bend controls.
            • The icon strip
            • Spice and Dice vary the current arpeggio or sequence; their effects are audible only while the Arpeggiator or Sequencer runs. Dice changes gates and triggers, while Spice sets the amount of variation.
            • Spice & Dice
            • Microfreak/UG/10 Keyboard explains the Icon strip in detail.

Microfreak/UG/12 Arpeggiator/03 Arpeggio Rate/01 Using Sync

  • 12.3.1 Using Sync

    • Sync coordinates the timing of modules or connected instruments. The MicroFreak can sync to a DAW or external synthesizer, with Rate setting divisions such as double speed or half speed.
    • The LFO, Arpeggiator, and Sequencer can sync to the clock. Their synchronized rhythms can add accents and rhythmic shifts.

Microfreak/UG/13 Sequencer/01 Use/02 Keyboard

  • 13.1.2. The Sequencer and the Keyboard

    • In monophonic mode, holding a keyboard key pauses a playing sequence. The sequence resumes when you release the key. In paraphonic mode, it continues alongside your playing.
    • The sequencer can play Unison voices or trigger chords in Chord Mode. Voice priority is keyboard notes, then incoming MIDI notes, then sequencer notes.
